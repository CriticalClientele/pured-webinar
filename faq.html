Frequently Asked Questions

Free live masterclass with Max · Friday, October 23 · Live on Zoom · 6:00 PM CT

Do I need followers?

Not to get started. UGC is usually made for the brand to use in its own ads and pages, not posted to your account. Some brands do prefer creators with an audience, and Max will cover what to expect.

Do I need to be on camera?

Not always. Some UGC is hands-only product demos, unboxings, or voiceovers recorded on your phone.

I work full-time. Is this for me?

It can be. UGC is made on your own schedule, so you can learn and film around a job. The masterclass is free, so you can see if it fits. Time needed and results vary.

Is it really free?

Yes. The masterclass is free to attend, with a live Q&A at the end. Max will also share information about Pured Media’s paid coaching program. There is no obligation to buy. You’ll get the Zoom link by email after you register.

Will there be a replay?

Replays are not guaranteed. Join live so you can ask questions.
